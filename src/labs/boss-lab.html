The Black Wick

What the cold flame left behind.

Wick Hero's shadow. Same body, same stance, same katana, drawn with Wick Hero's own animation code and recoloured live: black wax, a crown of dark red fire, a crimson scarf, a black blade whose edge burns red, and two red eyes. Wisps rise off him all fight; the pit is sunk in red haze with ash falling.

How to find him

In the lab he is always available: press ??? · The Black Wick under Start. In the game: clear Chapter 3 without falling once, and when the abyss opens a black crack tears open in the air beside it. Jump into it. The Characters (Beta) screen also has a door to the duel.

How he fights

A duellist. No contact damage and no stun to earn: he takes normal hits, but he is out of reach for much of each attack (blinking, vanishing, guarding). Every attack ends in an open window (blade low, breathing) — that is the safe time to strike. Strike into his guard and he punishes you.

Attacks and their tells

  • Draw cut: he crouches with a hand on the hilt and a red glint flares at the scabbard with a thin "tsing". He blinks through you at chest height and leaves a red line hanging in the air, which splits a beat later. Jump it. Below half he cuts twice, back and forth.
  • Combo: blade raised overhead, eyes flare white-hot; if you are far he closes the gap during the raise. Three cuts and a cross, stepping in. Back off or jump to a ledge; he is open afterwards.
  • Moonfall: he leaps and vanishes in black smoke. A red column of light marks you, and he drops through it with a falling cut. Three times, four below half. Reaches the ledges too.
  • Blood crescents: he steps back with the blade held low and glowing, then throws flying sword-slash crescents along the floor. Jump them. Below half the last is thrown from a raised blade and flies high: stay down for that one.
  • Guard: blade held level, edge burning a steady red, a ringing hum. Strike it and he deflects, blinks behind you and cuts. Wait it out and he lowers it, open.
  • Severance (below half): the room goes dark, a dashed red sphere marks where you stand, and cuts fill it while he slowly sheathes. At the click every cut lands for two embers. Get out of the sphere.

Below half

His crown blazes, every tell is about 20% shorter, and Severance comes in every fourth attack. If you stand on a ledge he mostly answers with Moonfall.

Numbers

48 HP (Wick Hero does 3 a hit, Wick 1). Health, rest between attacks and tell length are on the sliders below once he is unlocked.

The Kiln Pit

A white wax bridge on two pillars, 32 tiles wide. Under it is an air gap, then open lava. The wyrm swims half in the lava, its shelled back breaking the surface in humps like a dolphin, so you always see where it is and when it coils to strike. Wax crates, jars and candles stand on the deck and hop whenever the bridge shakes (looks only).

How the fight explains itself

  • A dark shell covers its body and head. Blades and bolts clang, flash a white ring and shove you back.
  • One plug of white wax seals the top of its skull, the same wax as the floors you dive through. It is always on show, but the head rides too high to reach with a normal jump. Only the quake's throw gets you above it.
  • Down-slash on the plug bounces you; landing on it bounces you. Only a Wax Dive breaks it.
  • Broken open, the skull glows and the shell seams blaze. Now blades bite.

Stage 1 · Tallow Wyrm

  • Quake (the main opening): it surfaces a dash away from you and rears high while the deck rattles, then smashes its head down on the bridge. Everything on it is thrown high, you included, and you hang in the air for about a second and a half. Steer or dash over its head and dive onto the plug. Miss and you land; the head is too high to reach from the deck. Below half it slams twice.
  • Bite: you see it swim under you and the planks glow and crack. It bursts up with its jaws gaping wide, lunges a little toward you and snaps them shut. Step away once the glow stops following you.
  • Flamethrower: it surfaces away from you and draws back to breathe in (mouth shut, eyes narrowed, throat glowing), then throws its head forward and roars with its eyes blown white. Brows slammed down, it pours billowing pixel fire that spreads along the bridge, holds and dies back, then hisses smoke. The fire only burns the wax bridge: a cornered player can step off onto the stone bank. But camp on a bank and it rears out on the bridge, roars, and lobs the fire onto the bank (the stone glows first); step back onto the bridge.
  • Leap: it arcs out of the lava over the bridge and back in. Below half it lands with walls of fire.
  • Stunned 3.2 s; then it quakes no more for two attacks.

Pupa → Stage 2 · Cinder Moth

  • The hatch (about 7.5 s, controls locked): the camera pushes in, the room darkens and bars close in under a low droning hum. A heartbeat speeds up, red veins swell on the shell and something presses outward from inside. The lights gutter out for a moment and two red eyes are all you see. Legs punch through, the shell splits, and the moth crawls out in jerky stops, still tied to the husk by wet strands, its head twitching. Then it goes still and stares at you in the dark before the wings snap open and it screams.
  • Look: darker, torn wings with moth-eaten holes; the wing spots are pale staring eyes whose pupils follow you; extra small red eyes on the head; a curled proboscis that straightens when it strikes; grey fur; random head twitches.
  • Triple slash (new): it closes in at your height and three green lights appear over it. Each light flares, and the wing glints green, just before its slash lands. Hit it while a light burns and the slash breaks: a green flash, and it drops stunned. Only your first strike counts. Strike at the wrong moment and the lights turn red, so you have to dodge the rest.
  • Rush: wings cocked, eyes flare, a red sight line to you, the aim locks, then it rushes along it. Now twice in a row (three times below half). After the last one it climbs back low and slow: dive on it.
  • Pounce: hovers over you while its shadow grows dark red, drops, fire rolls out both ways, then it rests on the floor with its saddle in reach.
  • Ember rain is gone from this fight. Call moths stays for wax; the fire sweep joins below half.

For discussion

  • Quake difficulty. In a bot test the dive lands when you dash about a third of a second after the bounce and dive just past it. Is that window right, or should the quake bounce higher?
  • Should the quake hurt if you are already in the air when it hits, or stay harmless?

  • The bridge props. More of them, or different things (chains, lanterns)?
