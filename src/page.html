Controls

Move
← → or A D
Jump
Space or Z. Hold for a higher jump. ↓ + Jump drops through roots.
Slash
X or J. Hold ↑ to strike up, ↓ in the air to strike down.
Dash
Shift, C or K. One dash in the air.
Focus
Hold F while standing to melt 33 wax into one ember.
Skill
E or L. Hold ↑ or ↓ for the other skills once learned. Each costs 33 wax.
Rest
↑ at a shrine
Pause
P or Esc. M toggles sound. Gamepads work too.
Phone
Touch and drag anywhere on the left to move. Jump, slash, dash and focus sit on the right. Full screen always plays sideways. The sliders icon lets you move and resize every button.

Field notes

  • New here? The tutorial is one short room: move, jump, dash, slash, focus and bounce. Experienced players can skip straight to Wickhollow from hero select or the pause menu.
  • Embers are your health. Wick starts with five. The vessel at top left holds wax.
  • Wick swings a wide, flat crescent that covers space above, below and ahead, and alternates its stroke each swing.
  • Wick Hero is the easiest. Nine embers, a long reach, huge damage and double wax from every hit. Its fourth strike, Severance, fills a wide sphere ahead with cuts.
  • Lampknight is the easier hero. Seven embers, longer reach, and a four-hit combo whose last strike deals double damage. Slower on foot and in the dash.
  • Every hit you land adds wax. Three hits is a third of the vessel, enough to Focus once.
  • Strike down to bounce. A down-slash on a foe or on spines springs you back up and refreshes your dash.
  • Spines cost one ember and return you to the last solid ground.
  • Coins buy skills. Trade 3 coins at a blacksmith. Chapter 1's smith (end of the Drip) forges Ember Bolt; Chapter 2's forges Wax Dive. Skill damage grows with your hero's slash.
  • Music. "Beneath A Frozen Horizon": its calm opening loops at camp and in the stages; its climax takes over at each boss's roar and fades back after. Muffled when paused. M mutes everything.
  • Gallery. From the home screen: pick any hero, play every move on a button press, spawn either boss and trigger each of its attacks or its entrance.
  • Focus is a risk. Wax drains while you hold it. Let go early and it flows back; take a hit and it spills.
  • Shrines keep your place. Rest at one to restore every ember and set where you return.
