# Changelog

Wickfall is in beta. Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`: a patch fixes or rebalances, a minor version adds content, 1.0.0 leaves beta.

## v0.1.2-beta · 2026-10-19

- Touch: the Skill button now sits next to Jump and Dash, so a Wax Dive in mid-air is one short slide; Focus moves to Skill's old spot. Layouts you arranged yourself are kept.
- Touch: the Arrange buttons icon no longer slips off the right edge when touch controls first appear on the title screen.

## v0.1.1-beta · 2026-10-19

Fewer places to get stuck.

- Tips that pause the game and play the move with Wick: lighting a brazier, diving through white wax, stunning the Tallow Wyrm and the Cinder Moth (after two falls), and every new skill.
- Arrows point at cold braziers and at the Wyrm's cap and the Moth's back while you are close.
- Bigger, longer hint text.
- A hit that knocks you into spines no longer costs a second ember; you return away from foes, with longer protection after a return or a fall.

## v0.1.0-beta · 2026-10-19

First public beta.

- Three chapters, four keepers, three heroes, a boss rush and a hidden duel.
- Phones always play full screen, sideways.
- New Black Wick slash sounds.
